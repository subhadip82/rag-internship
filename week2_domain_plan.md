# Week 2 Domain Plan

## 1. Project Title
Sustainable Travel Advisor: Local Eco-Friendly Itineraries

## 2. Target Users
- Eco-conscious tourists planning trips in major cities
- Sustainable travel bloggers and content creators
- Local tour guides and small travel businesses offering green experiences
- Urban travelers seeking low-impact transport and activity options

## 3. Problem Statement
Travelers who want to plan sustainable trips struggle to assemble accurate, locally relevant recommendations from diverse sources. Existing guides are often outdated, inconsistent, or too general, making it hard to discover eco-friendly lodging, transport, dining, and experiences that align with sustainability goals.

## 4. Documents to be Used
- City sustainability reports and green tourism guides
- Local eco-friendly hotel and accommodation listings
- Public transit and bike-share route summaries
- Responsible dining and farmers market directories
- Environmental certification criteria for tourism businesses

## 5. Five Expected Questions
1. What are the best eco-friendly neighborhoods to stay in for a weekend visit?
2. Which low-emission transport options are available for getting around the city sustainably?
3. Can you recommend green restaurants or cafes that use local, organic ingredients?
4. What eco-conscious outdoor activities or wellness experiences are available nearby?
5. How can I find certified sustainable hotels and accommodations in this city?

## 6. Questions the System Should Not Answer
- Questions outside the city or region covered by the provided documents
- Personal medical, legal, or financial advice unrelated to sustainable travel
- Requests for exact pricing, live availability, or future bookings
- Questions about unrelated topics such as politics, sports, or entertainment
- Technical questions about how the system itself is built or accesses data

## 7. Success Criteria
- The system identifies and cites relevant local sustainability documents for each travel question.
- Answers are practical, grounded in the provided materials, and clearly focus on eco-friendly travel.
- The output guides users to sustainable lodging, transport, dining, and activity options without making unsupported claims.
- The project folder structure is ready for Week 3 implementation, with a clear domain plan and source data organization.
